Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Crafting Battle (package name com.craftingbattle.game, the "Game") handles information. The Game is developed and published by [DEVELOPER / STUDIO NAME] ("we", "us").

In short
  • There are no accounts or logins. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or photos.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, such as the advertising ID, to show and measure ads.
  • You can reset ad personalisation, change your consent choice, or delete your game data at any time.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress in a local file on your device. This includes your level, stars, coins, diamonds, unlocked and upgraded weapons, daily reward and quest progress, and settings (music, sound, vibration). This data is not sent to us, and we cannot see it.

You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the Game, or by uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data in your device settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game offers optional rewarded video ads. You can choose to watch one, for example, to revive your wall, double your rewards or get a free reroll. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (country or city);
  • ad interactions (ads viewed or clicked) and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. To learn how Google uses it, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the first time you open the Game, you may see Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). It lets you accept or refuse personalised ads. You can still play and use rewarded ads if you refuse; the ads will then be non-personalised.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads), depending on your device.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. The Game can be played without watching any ad.

We (the developer) do not operate servers for the Game. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. If you contact us by email, we will use your email address and message only to reply to you.

5. Children

The Game is not specifically directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help delete it.

6. Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the Game. Data handled by Google AdMob is kept under Google's own retention policies. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests are best handled by the ad controls described above, or directly with Google. You can also contact us at any time.
